Our Story

Part of This Community Since 1977.

At the Good Sport, we strive to provide quality service, good selection, and fair pricing — in sports gear for kids of all ages.

One of our sweet spots is helping parents of young players choose quality equipment that is reasonably priced and up-to-date. Whether it's lacrosse, soccer, baseball, or swimming — we maintain an inventory to suit all your athletic equipment needs.

For five consecutive years we've been named Best Sporting Goods Store on the South Shore by both the Mariner Newspapers and South Shore Magazine. We are deeply active in our local communities, donating time and resources to youth sports programs.

⭐ Best of South Shore — Mariner Newspapers ⭐ Best of South Shore — South Shore Magazine

— Austin Lyne, Owner
